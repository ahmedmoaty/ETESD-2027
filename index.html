01 / About the conference

Where engineering ideas meet real-world challenges.

A scientific gathering built around research exchange, practical application, and cross-disciplinary collaboration.

Welcome to the Scientific Conference on Emerging Trends in Engineering Research, Technological Applications and Sustainable Development (ETESD 2027).

ETESD 2027 aims to bring together researchers, academics, teaching assistants, young scientists, engineers, and students from various engineering disciplines.

The conference provides a platform for exchanging knowledge, presenting research findings, exploring technological advancements, and discussing innovative engineering solutions that contribute to sustainable development.

Through oral presentations, poster sessions, and engineering project demonstrations, ETESD 2027 seeks to foster scientific discussion, knowledge sharing, research networking, and the integration of scientific research with practical engineering applications.

Our Vision

Research without boundaries.

To establish a collaborative scientific platform that promotes engineering innovation, interdisciplinary research, and technological solutions supporting sustainable development.

Our Mission

Knowledge into application.

To encourage the exchange of scientific knowledge, support emerging researchers, and connect engineering research with practical applications through presentations, technical discussions, and interdisciplinary collaboration.

03 / Call for abstracts

Bring your research to the conversation.

The ETESD 2027 Organizing Committee invites academics, researchers, teaching assistants, engineers, and students to submit research abstracts and engineering project summaries.

CATEGORY A / ORAL
↗

Research Presentations

Present scientific investigations, methodologies, findings, and engineering contributions, followed by discussion with other participants.

CATEGORY B / POSTER
▤

Poster Presentations

Showcase research objectives, methods, results, and conclusions through visual scientific posters and interactive discussions.

CATEGORY C / PROJECT
⚙

Engineering Projects

Demonstrate engineering projects, prototypes, technical applications, and innovative approaches to real-world challenges.

Submission guidelines

Prepare your abstract

  • Language: English
  • Abstract length: 200–300 words
  • Clear, concise research title
  • Full names of all contributing authors
  • Department or affiliation, where applicable
  • 3–5 keywords
  • Background, objectives, and methodology
  • Main findings or preliminary results and conclusions

For ongoing research and projects, clearly identify the development stage and expected outcomes.

Review & presentation

What happens next?

  • Choose the most relevant scientific track.
  • Select your preferred presentation category.
  • Prepare and submit your abstract or project summary.
  • The Scientific Committee reviews submissions for relevance, clarity, technical content, innovation, and practical significance.
  • Accepted participants are notified of their presentation format.
  • Prepare your oral presentation, poster, or demonstration.
Publication note: Acceptance is for presentation at ETESD 2027 and does not constitute publication in a peer-reviewed journal or indexed conference proceedings.

07 / Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Who can submit a research abstract?

ETESD 2027 welcomes submissions from academics, researchers, teaching assistants, engineers, undergraduate and postgraduate students working in relevant engineering fields.

Can I submit an engineering project instead of a research paper?

Yes. The conference includes an Engineering Projects category for prototypes, technical applications, and innovative engineering solutions. Submit a clear project summary identifying its objectives, methods, and outcomes.

Is submitting a full research paper required?

No. ETESD 2027 is designed for research abstracts, poster presentations, and engineering projects. The standard research abstract length is 200–300 words.

Will accepted abstracts be published in an indexed journal?

Acceptance is for presentation at the conference. It does not imply indexed publication or publication in a peer-reviewed journal.

Can a contribution involve more than one engineering discipline?

Yes. Interdisciplinary work is encouraged, especially research connecting emerging technologies, practical engineering applications, and sustainable development.
